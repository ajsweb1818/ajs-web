Special Collection

Snowball Earth

This Special Collection explores the dynamics, causes, and consequences of Cryogenian global glaciations and their aftermath, including climate dynamics, geochemical cycles, redox change, and links to early animal life.

View Articles Submission Information

Collection Overview

Organized as a focused collection within AJS, Snowball Earth brings together invited and submitted contributions on one of the most important topics in Earth history.

  • Theme: Cryogenian global glaciation and aftermath
  • Format: Special Collection
  • Journal: American Journal of Science
  • Article hosting: Scholastica

About this Collection

This collection highlights recent work on Snowball Earth events and their implications for Earth system evolution. Topics include the extent and dynamics of Cryogenian glaciation, deglaciation and CO2 buildup, ocean redox changes, geochemical cycling, sedimentary records, and possible links to early animal evolution.

The collection is intended to bring related work into conversation and to provide readers with a focused entry point into current research on Neoproterozoic global glaciation.
